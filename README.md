# The Google Developer Catch-22: How an Automated YouTube Ban Kills Independent Software Development (And Violates the DSA)

This repository contains the complete case timeline, documentation, and evidence regarding the termination of the YouTube channel `@KarelTest` and the subsequent lock-out of my Google Developer activities due to Google's automated "circumvention" policy.

---

## 🛑 The Core Issue (The Catch-22)

1. **The Verification Mandate:** To publish or update add-ons on the Google Workspace Marketplace (and optionally on the Chrome Web Store), developers are **required** to verify their OAuth consent screen. Part of this verification requires submitting an unlisted YouTube video showing the OAuth flow in action.
2. **The Automated Ban:** On August 15, 2025, my YouTube channel (which existed solely to host these developer verification videos) was terminated by an automated algorithm for "spam, deceptive practices, and scams" — triggered by the upload of a video demonstrating a Chrome Extension (*Google AI Studio - File Reader*).
3. **The Permanent Lock-out:** Because of YouTube's "circumvention policy," any new channel or account I attempt to use is immediately terminated. I am now permanently blocked from submitting verification videos, effectively ending my ability to publish and update software in the Google ecosystem.

---

## 🔍 Internal Escalation Attempts (All Exhausted)

Before seeking out-of-court settlement and regulatory action, I exhausted every possible internal support and escalation channel within Google and YouTube over a multi-week period:

1. **Automated Appeals:** Submitted multiple appeals explaining the developer context. All were rejected within minutes by automated systems.
2. **Public Social Media:** Contacted `@TeamYouTube` on X (Twitter), only to receive boilerplate responses pointing back to the automated appeals.
3. **Physical Registered Mail:** Sent a formal physical letter to `Google Ireland Limited` in Dublin detailing the developer blockages.
4. **Legal Support Contact:** Emailed `legal@support.youtube.com`, which resulted in automated responses redirecting to the standard consumer appeals page.
5. **Cross-Platform Support:** Opened tickets with **Chrome Web Store Developer Support** and attempted to reach **Google Cloud Platform Support**, but both stated they could not help with YouTube accounts, completing the Catch-22 loop.
6. **Developer Relations:** Reached out to Google Workspace Developer Relations (DevRel), which went unanswered.

## ⚖️ Why this is a DSA Violation

Google has systematically failed to comply with the European Union's **Digital Services Act (DSA)** (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065):

* **Article 17 (Statement of Reasons):** Google has refused to provide a clear, specific, and individual reason detailing *which* video or *what* behavior triggered the ban, preventing any meaningful defense.
* **Article 20 (Internal Complaint Handling):** Appeals are rejected within minutes by automated systems without human review.
* **Article 21 (Out-of-Court Dispute Settlement):** The case was submitted to the certified Out-of-Court Dispute Settlement body *Appeals Centre Europe* (ACE). Google ignored the proceedings entirely, resulting in two default decisions in my favor (Cases `#2025-012481-YT` and `#2025-012487-YT`) which Google now refuses to execute.
